# Mechanical verification

| Rule | Enforcement |
| --- | --- |
| Bind each screw and nut to its declared seat; another valid seat is not equivalent. | `resin_retention.Joint`, `check_axes`, `check_joints`; `check_resin_retention.py` swaps nuts and relocates complete pairs in helpers and full candidates. |
| Export hardware from Joint records; do not maintain a separate mutable hardware list. | `joint_hardware` and `resin_base.candidate_hardware` derive the actual exported solids. |
| An internal bar radius does not establish outer ball-shell clearance. | `below_plane_buffer.below_root_guard` checks every fixed solid against the operator-confirmed opening plane; the whole-candidate old-cap mutant must fail. Physical fit remains a separate gate. |
| Keep at least 0.5 mm PTFE under every seated countersunk head, and derive trials from the exact full source. | `below_plane_buffer.verify_seated_head_tolerances`, material-web guard, old 0.09 mm recipe regression, and `buffer_ear_trial` two-way source material checks. |
| Bind trial provenance to captured receipt bytes and the complete producer set. | `ReceiptSnapshot`, exact `SOURCE_PATHS` inventory, repeated input/output checks; `check_buffer_ear_trial.py` rejects omitted sources and receipt/artifact mutations across build, verification and export. |
| Derive C2 ports and service clearance from the actual paired exports, including whole-tool staging. | `resin_c2` validates the immutable eight-source producer and all states/sweeps, bounds every above-roof fixed part, and checks actual planar support. `check_resin_c2.py` restores unported panels/old tiedowns and rejects insufficient lift and unbounded fixed material. |
| Preserve the complete inclined roof, mounting seats, axial reserve and closed ballast capsule when adding outlet choices. | `arcade_dual_exit` binds actual v4 exports, keeps the rear outlet above the intact tray and checks both routes with the cover installed; its checker rejects blocked routes and real roof/seat/capsule/corridor mutations. |
| State the real hex-top datum when well depth differs from estimated nut thickness, and keep loading channels open through deeper seats. | `measured_hex_joint.Parameters.hex_top_z`, matching keeper nose and explicit loading-bottom argument; its checker rejects missing nose, old dimensions, capped nut path and shifted axis. |
| Move compact latch clearance below the roof without introducing bar-to-box retention or losing the hex load path. | `compact_mount` bounds every fixed solid, retains 5 mm lower webs, checks actual C2/tools and assembly B at g5/g7; its checker rejects solid roofs, missing webs/noses, blocked loading, wrong shims/lengths and insufficient lift. Default, optional and trial inventories remain separate. |
| A rotary/base clearance certificate does not admit a post-glue coupling lifecycle. | `post_glue_base_gate` requires actual all-angle containment and full tool phases with separate base/mouth frames; native failures are fatal, old C2 lifecycle is rejected, and fresh proof namespaces cannot overwrite prior data. `check_post_glue_candidate_base` binds actual stage exports; the separate lifecycle verifier must prove every attachment/reverse from bare glued bar. |
| C tool identifiers must preserve the floor, cable reserve and complete two-key workflow. | `c_ready_mount` binds final C and actual complete tool phases to the shared service index; `check_c_ready_mount.py` rejects the real cable-channel label placement, deep engraving, wrong index and a connected blocked port. Only shallow bottom markings may differ from the frozen compact base. |
| Current C service removes the blank bottom while wiring and sealed ballast stay on the fixed shell. | `c_panel_service_mount` checks actual panel-off tool obstacles, closed-panel rotation, purchased-washer capture through full play, fixed strain relief, and the 66-state composed protocol. `check_c_panel_service_mount.py` rejects an undersized washer, missing fixed eye and closed-panel tool service. The marked lid remains historical. |
