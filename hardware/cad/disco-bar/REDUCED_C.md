# Reduced C implementation boundary

One candidate preserves C456's immutable actual bar, cup, shoes, pads, M4 lock and driver interface. It fuses the cover to half B, fills its former 15/310-degree fastener posts and removes those two M3 pairs. A negative-X 35 mm capsule extends the cover's existing driver clearance for external assembly. The original C source and artifacts remain unchanged.

The new physical inventory is six printed pieces including the bar, ten purchased metal pieces and two cut pads: 17 detachable actors. One identical shoe design is printed twice, giving five unique printable designs. Separate actor instances remain in assembly verification.

`check_reduced_c.py --baseline ORIGINAL_C456_RECEIPT --output OUTPUT` pins and validates the original actual files, exports only the new fused component, and rechecks all five actual assembly endpoints. It does not admit the design.

New motion/contact/protocol evidence must check A with preloaded M4 and M3 nuts first, then B with its integral cover around the fixed driver, then the two remaining M3 screws. Complete reversal occurs outside the already-glued ball. Uncapped M4 head/nut capture and a seated-head condition during cover insertion are independent checks, not assumptions from the old capped module.

`verification/reduced_c_admission.py::load(config)` validates the exact candidate, independent 17-actor inventory, material and continuous motion proofs, fresh lifecycle and mutation controls. It returns a local candidate admission requiring combined base admission. The Box owner separately binds all-angle clearance, complete ordinary tools, mounted grip access, module withdrawal and the service-panel graph to the newly frozen base. Neither local admission nor an endpoint receipt authorizes publication or fabrication.

The local verification run has 35 reachable states and 68 enabled transitions. Every reachable state can return to the bare glued bar. It uses fresh evidence for the 17 actors rather than importing the old C lifecycle count. Physical fit, printed strength and grip slip resistance remain unverified.
