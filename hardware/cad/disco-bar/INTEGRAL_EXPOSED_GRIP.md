# Integral exposed opposed-shoe concept

This concept replaces the cup, collar, cover and M4 latch with one printed extension of the complete original bar. The two existing rigid shoes, two cut pads, two M3 set screws and two M3 nuts are reused geometrically. There are three physical rotor prints from two designs: integral bar and two shoes. With the new mounting shell and standard bottom cover, the operator's target is five physical prints. No old hex-well keepers belong to this arrangement.

The exploratory exposure is 22 mm below the original ball-opening plane. Side screw axes are 9 mm below that plane, and the complete shoes occupy 2 to 16 mm below it. The conical foot grows from the original 47.2306651 mm radius to a proposed 51 mm radius. These are design parameters, not hardware measurements. A straight full-width extension was considered, but the taper gives the same one-piece grip without the extra outer material.

The complete bar retains all original material, exterior insertion profile and 109.22 mm attachment reach. Original debond lumens remain unchanged and continue through the exposed foot. The current stop option intentionally fills the unused central original socket void and recuts the known 25.4 mm pocket at the exposed mouth. This gives a nominal top stop at source Z -3.4 mm; it does not preserve negative-Z material equality because original void is deliberately filled. A thin disk would seal an inaccessible old cavity and create an FDM roof. Sparse ribs would assume unknown knob-top bearing details. The filled-core option adds no separate part.

The operator clarified that about 3/32 inch (2.38125 mm) is the stationary flange's axial thickness, with no visible gap above it. The earlier gap interpretation is invalid. The nominal roof contacts the flange underside at world Z0, while flange top and knob underside are at world Z2.38125. Assuming the nominal 25.4 mm knob height, the E22 bar root is at world Z24.38125 and its foot ends at world Z2.38125. The fixed flange remains inside the nominal central bore rather than under the outer foot annulus. These are exploratory placement statements, not verified installed clearances or tolerances.

The actual knob height and top bearing shape matter. With the relocated stop seated, bar-root height is T + E + (Hactual - 25.4), and foot height above the roof is T + (Hactual - 25.4). A shorter knob can reduce nominal roof clearance. Original socket depth is not a measurement of the knob. Base clearance, full stationary housing geometry, spindle support, physical fit, pad friction, screw preload, adhesive and strength require separate checks.

## Hollow column and slicer density

The original far column remains hollow. The native addition check permits new material only inside the old central knob socket, so it does not fill the rest of the 109.22 mm column. A valid single CAD solid means a closed manufacturable boundary; it does not mean 100% filament infill. The localized filled-core CAD region gives the new stop a continuous supporting volume and avoids a sealed inaccessible cavity. Slicer walls, infill and possible local reinforcement at the stop, nut reactions and grip walls remain unspecified until printer/material and physical tests are available. No blanket 100% infill instruction or percentage is selected here.

## Verified intrinsic geometry

`integral_exposed_grip.py` exports the earlier no-stop concept with the entire original negative-Z bar unchanged. Its v2 STEP/STL files remain intact. That variant has no new stop at the proposed knob top and holds height through pad friction. It is retained as a comparison, not silently changed into the stop option.

`integral_exposed_grip_stop.py` creates a separate filled-core stop concept. `check_integral_exposed_grip_stop.py` reimports the nine actual components and checks solid and mesh validity, adaptive STEP/STL volumes and bounds, retention of all original material, addition only within the old socket void, original channel material/void equality, extended lumen clearance, relocated pocket and complete floor. A positive ideal-cylinder overtravel witnesses nominal stop contact; it does not prove contact with the actual knob top. Actual shortened-bar, plugged-lumen and removed-floor STEP mutants must reject, then the restored positive is rerun.

`check_integral_grip_service.py` checks continuous intrinsic assembly and reversal on the actual new body. Both nuts feed through the exposed mouth and slide radially into their pockets. Positive witnesses check outward nut reaction, gravity support, anti-turn faces and the installed shoe's inward reaction. Bonded shoes/pads enter axially while moved inward, then seat radially. Released and preset positions differ by a proposed 0.2 mm radial stroke; M3 pitch 0.5 mm gives 0.4 nominal screw turn. The released whole module clears a nominal socket-radius knob approach. Full nominal 100 by 20 mm L-key envelopes cover exposed-side entry, lower-half operation/reindexing and withdrawal. Actual key dimensions, stock screw hex engagement and physical compression remain unverified.

`verification/integral_grip_protocol.py` independently reconstructs the exact eight detachable actors. Its instructed intrinsic lifecycle has 17 reachable states and 32 transitions, each reversible to all actors outside the already-glued integral bar. Six negative controls reject preinstalled hardware, omitted hardware, missing unlock, above-root access, a custom tool and an unsupported ball. External pad bonding guards the whole module outside. Hold the ball during both sequential side-key operations and until both grips are set; removal first restores the holding hand. This proves the stated discrete instructions, not physical grip or installed stack safety.
