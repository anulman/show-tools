# Post-glue coupling prototypes

These proposals assemble through the root after the bare one-piece bar has been glued inside the ball. They supersede the rejected C2 retention scheme. None is a promoted manufacturing design.

- A uses three M4 bolts with nuts that enter keyholes, turn 35 degrees and seat in integral hex pockets.
- B uses three printed bayonet lugs and one M4 locking bolt.
- C keeps the padded cup stationary while a removable two-piece collar turns 35 degrees. Its M4 lock enters at 110 degrees and seats at 145 degrees; its integral drive starts at 260 degrees before turning. Four ordinary M3 cover joints close the collar around the cup outside the ball, with two distinct stations on each half. The earlier M2 joints are removed.

Each M4 clamp moves 2.1 mm over three turns at 0.7 mm pitch. Ordinary cap screws and a printed head-capture cover keep the released assembly together. The cover is functional retention hardware. Do not replace it with a reduced-neck screw or washer without a new capture and access proof.

C has axial collar play from -0.5 to +0.6 mm relative to the cup. Normal exported operating poses explicitly hold the upper stop at -0.5 mm. Its 3 mm key must bottom on the socket floor at source Z4.5 to transmit axial force through the head seat to that stop. A floating key at Z4.7 does not establish that force path. The 4 mm key also bottoms at source Z4.5 in its printed driver; its whole 140 mm body and 25 mm short arm are contained in every entry, operating and withdrawal envelope. The actual STEP race checker bounds the complete released 1.1 mm race travel and 0.5 mm threaded head float at fixed nut orientation, with positive upper/lower-stop, head-seat and cover-capture witnesses. Rotation holds the upper stop with a bottomed 4 mm key. The continuous root proof includes 0.5 mm captured screw float during that turn, with the nut remaining 0.1 mm below the actual bar lip. Seating and clamping require a bottomed 3 mm key and seated head. The race bounds do not authorize rotation with a free collar or free nut spin. Operating contact conditions require lifecycle admission.

C's M4 nut enters radially before the screw is inserted. An axial nut approach crosses the fixed upper race. The cup capture races and collar have separate entry reliefs for the integral M4 seat. On the bench, assemble half A with the common cover, M4 pair and 130/200-degree M3 joints first; slide that complete group around the cup, then slide half B and its preloaded nuts under the cover and install the 15/310-degree screws. Reverse those two screws and half B before withdrawing the A group. Matched continuous group bounds and actual halfspace/height checks cover this order. All cover hardware, grip hardware and final dismantling remain outside the ball. Normal ball removal leaves the M4 nuts threaded onto their screws.

The original bare-bar profile outside the bounded mouth and both original fluid channels remain protected. Debond access is established after the removable module is withdrawn. Full straight extensions of those channels through the installed module are not guaranteed during actuation.

## Verification

Run the generator/export check with `check_post_glue_candidates.py --step ORIGINAL.step --concept A|B|C --output OUTPUT`. Run actual-artifact assembly checks with `check_post_glue_external.py --source OUTPUT/receipt.json --output PROOF` in the isolated CAD Python environment. Use `check_post_glue_preservation.py --source OUTPUT/receipt.json --reference ORIGINAL.step --output STATIC.json` for actual-file profile, channel, socket and blind-floor checks. Use `check_post_glue_contacts.py --source OUTPUT/receipt.json --output CONTACTS` for actual nut floor, head seat, capture and positive drive witnesses. For C, use `check_post_glue_c_root_motion.py --source OUTPUT/receipt.json --output ROOT` for full insertion, 35-degree turn, 2.1 mm seating/clamp and reverse paths. Use `check_post_glue_race.py --source OUTPUT/receipt.json --output RACE` for released race/head-float bounds and contact witnesses. Source hashes are captured before each run and checked again before its receipt is written.

The delivered STEP solids are authoritative for requirement checks. Some C source-to-STEP Boolean comparisons classify an entire part as difference in one direction while returning zero in the other. Raw signed mass is positive and infinity classifies OUT for both solids; two imports of the same STEP compare empty in both directions. Changing STEP precision, pcurves, split-plane numerical normal, or a temporary split offset did not resolve the source comparison. Its positive results remain recorded as INCONCLUSIVE, never zero or equivalence PASS. Validity, adaptive mass and STL checks do not replace actual STEP geometry, motion, contact and lifecycle proofs.

Endpoint clearance and external assembly/tool bounds are separate milestones. Final admission also needs original-profile/channel/socket checks on actual files, continuous root insertion/turn/clamp/removal, C's race/contact states, unlimited installed rotation against the actual base, and a reversible lifecycle graph bound to those artifacts. Physical fit, preload, grip strength, material durability and adhesive strength remain unverified.

Frozen receipts under ignored build storage are active review evidence. Preserve them and the original input until a self-contained editable design checkpoint and manufacturing handoff have been verified under `docs/data-retention.md`.
