# Air-gap C service design

- [x] Ground: the reduced C has 17 detachable actors and an immutable bar. Its captured collar can move from source delta -0.5 to +0.6 mm, with a further 0 to 0.5 mm captured M4 head float. Existing rotation requires the upper stop; released axial travel is already checked at both entry and turned angles.
- [x] Sketch: exchange ordinary AF4 and AF3 keys serially while the other hand holds the ball. Remove the first key completely, recover the second key's socket, seat its floor and restore the collar upper stop before any turn or clamp operation. No ball rest on the base and no simultaneous keys are permitted.
- [x] Agree: coordinator selected serial exchange over temporary packing strips. Preserve every C part and actual export.
- [x] Implement: new full-key recovery evidence and a separate protocol/admission bind the unchanged actual geometry. Box owns the new base, exact placement, positive gap and combined access/rotation proofs.
- [x] Verify locally: strict native continuous envelopes, original subject fingerprints, fresh reachable-state invariants and real negative controls.
- [ ] Combined admission: Box must still verify the new base, every-angle clearance, full tools, mounted grip, module withdrawal and panel service at both accepted placements.

The public boundary is `airgap_c_admission.load(config)`, returning the unchanged candidate plus the new model, independent 17-actor inventory, lifecycle, source pins and full ordinary-tool recovery report. It requires a separate new-base admission. Historical reduced-C admission remains immutable geometry evidence; its PTFE-rest protocol does not describe air-gap service.

The new geometry checker treats the moving collar and fixed cup/bar in separate frames. AF3 socket recovery spans up to 1.6 mm in the fixed cup frame (1.1 mm collar travel plus 0.5 mm head float), but only 0.5 mm relative to the collar. AF4 recovery spans 1.1 mm in the fixed frame and zero relative to its own drive socket. Withdrawal is coordinated: the key retreats at least as far as a following collar; the captured lower stop bounds follow travel. The tool cannot be assumed stationary while the collar passes through its round stem.

Assembly after the 35-degree turn is: hold ball, release and withdraw AF4, engage AF3, seat the head and push to the upper stop, lift ball 2.1 mm into the nut register, then tighten three turns while lowering the ball by that stroke. Removal reverses this with AF3 removed before AF4 is introduced. The ball remains hand-held whenever the clamp is not secured. Unrestricted assembled rotation is checked against the new base separately. Physical grip, spindle load capacity, hand reach, fit and strength remain unverified.


Run `check_airgap_c_service.py --source REDUCED_C_RECEIPT --output FRESH` in the existing CAD environment, then `check_airgap_c_controls.py` with the same arguments. The control fills the actual turned AF4 socket with connected material, requires a mechanical collision rejection and reruns the complete positive service proof after restoring original inputs. All temporary boolean operands and exported witnesses are detached copies. Exact non-mesh BRep fingerprints confirm the actual parts remain unchanged.

Freeze with `verification/freeze_airgap_c.py --geometry-admission ORIGINAL_LOCAL_ADMISSION --service NEW_SERVICE_RECEIPT --controls NEW_CONTROL_RECEIPT --output FRESH`. The loader validates both producer and current consumer source bytes. It reconstructs and verifies the new protocol and its hand/stop invariants independently; the old PTFE-rest model is not reused. The new graph has no bar-to-box bearing edge. Original geometry proofs and their real negative controls remain bound because no C geometry changed.

The new local model has 35 reachable states and 68 transitions, coincidentally the same counts as the earlier protocol but a different model and digest. Ten protocol controls reject preinstalled hardware, missing unlock, side access, custom tools, omitted hardware, simultaneous keys, an unsupported ball, a loose collar turn, an old PTFE rest and omitted AF3 head recovery. Admission controls also reject the old protocol, altered source/proof hashes and omitted recovery evidence. A separate consumer-root replay passes.

The new base uses a nominal bar-root height of 5 mm, with a separately checked 7 mm adjustment and no height cap. The corresponding nominal knob-underside-to-stock-hex-top gaps are 5.25 and 7.25 mm; these are placement requirements within the confirmed adjustment range, not measurements of installed hardware. Collar parts extend into the central well, so bar-root height is not the minimum clearance of every moving part. That minimum belongs to the combined base proof.
